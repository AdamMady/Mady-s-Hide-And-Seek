# **THIS MOD ONLY NEEDS TO BE ON THE HOST! UNMODDED CLIENTS CAN PLAY!**



## Mod made by "AdamMady" you can join my discord here to look for codes, give me feedback or give me new mod ideas: https://discord.gg/5z3WvVhxCf



## If a player gets stuck tell them to rejoin!



## Features:



#### Only the host needs the mod! (It is recommended that other people in your friend group get the mod for better compatibility, but it is NOT a requirement!)



#### Gives seekers a little bell so you know if they're getting close!



#### Gives hiders random items! Ranging from flare guns to speakers!



#### 3 Preinstalled play areas!



#### Ability to create your own play area!



## Installation:



#### Step 1: Got BepInEx? Yes? Good! Go to step 2! No? Get it here: https://builds.bepinex.dev/projects/bepinex_be find the latest one with "IL2CPP" in the name, make sure it's the x64 version for your OS and extract to game directory (game local files) and give the game a quick launch and wait for a bit, When the game opens, close it and continue on to step 2!



#### Step 2: Just take the zip file, Throw it into the game directory, and unzip!



#### Step 3: Open the game, Press "Host Game", pick "4 Players", Press F9 to open the menu (And F9 when you want to close it), Click "Setup", Invite your friends, And then finally press "Start Round" when everyone's ready!



## Extra:



#### You can pick your own hiding areas by pressing F10 and pressing setting up border positions, recovery positions, and spawn positions, And finally deciding where the play area sign is!
